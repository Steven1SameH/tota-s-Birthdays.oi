VIDEO SETUP

1. Export your CapCut video as MP4.
2. Rename the file exactly:
   birthday-video.mp4
3. Put it inside this folder:
   assets/birthday-video.mp4
4. Open index.html with Live Server or upload the whole project to GitHub Pages.
5. Open the Cake section and click the cake image.

The site is already configured to play this local file. You do not need to upload the video here or to Google Drive.
